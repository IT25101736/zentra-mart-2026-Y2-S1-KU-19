**About Zentra Mart**
Zentra Mart is an online marketplace system designed to support the buying and selling of products through a digital platform.
The system focuses on key activities such as:

User account registration and management
Product listing and management
Product searching and filtering
Shopping cart and checkout
Payment processing
Order management
Order status updates
Administrative management
